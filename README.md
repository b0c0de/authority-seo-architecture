# Authority SEO Architecture 🏗️📈

**Scalable Topic Cluster templates, internal linking frameworks, and EEAT content checklists for affiliate, finance, and authority websites.**

Modern SEO is no longer about publishing isolated articles and hoping they rank. Search engines reward **structured content ecosystems** built around clear topical authority, strategic internal linking, and strong EEAT signals.

This repository contains battle-tested frameworks and templates developed over **20+ years of building high-volume content systems**.

* **[02. The Internal Linking Matrix](./02-internal-linking-matrix.md)** – Rules and anchor text distribution frameworks to pass link equity without over-optimization.
* **[03. EEAT & YMYL Checklist for Finance & Affiliate Sites](./03-eeat-finance-affiliate-checklist.md)** – Essential trust, editorial, and structural signals for YMYL, finance, and review websites.
