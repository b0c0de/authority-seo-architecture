# 02. The Internal Linking Matrix 🔗

Internal linking is the highest-leverage on-page SEO activity that most websites ignore. A strategic internal linking matrix distributes **PageRank (link equity)** from your strongest informational pages directly to your highest-converting commercial and affiliate pages.

---

### 1. The 3-Tier Link Flow Model

```text
[TIER 1: High-Traffic Informational Guides / Linkable Assets]
       │  (Passes authority & topical relevance)
       ▼
[TIER 2: Pillar Hubs & Category Comparison Pages]
       │  (Funneling warm, high-intent traffic)
       ▼
[TIER 3: Money Pages / Reviews / Affiliate & Lead-Gen URLs]
```

---

### 2. Anchor Text Distribution Framework

To avoid over-optimization penalties while maximizing keyword relevance, follow this anchor text ratio across your internal links:

| Anchor Type | Target Share | Example (Target Page: *Best Credit Cards for Travel*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exact Match** | 30% – 40% | `best credit cards for travel` |
| **Partial / Semantic Match** | 40% – 50% | `top travel rewards cards`, `comparing travel credit cards` |
| **Branded / Natural Phrase** | 10% – 20% | `our full travel card breakdown`, `see our top picks here` |

---

### 3. The 5 Golden Rules of Internal Linking

1. **Zero Orphan Pages:** Every indexable URL on your website must have at least **3 incoming internal links** from topically relevant pages.
2. **First Link Priority:** If a page links to the same destination URL twice, Google generally prioritizes the anchor text of the **first link** in the HTML. Make your first contextual link count.
3. **Link Early in the Content:** Contextual links placed within the first 200–300 words carry stronger user engagement and crawl priority than links buried in the footer.
4. **Cap Links Per Post:** Avoid stuffing 50 internal links into a 1,000-word post. Keep contextual keyword links to **1–2 per 300 words** for natural readability.
5. **Automate Repetitive Keyword Links:** Manually updating hundreds of older posts whenever you publish a new money page does not scale. Use **[Auto-Internal Linker for WordPress](https://github.com)** to automatically link target keywords across your entire archive with frequency limits.

---

### 4. Monthly Internal Link Audit Checklist

* [ ] Identify top 10 pages with the highest organic traffic or backlinks (via GSC / Ahrefs) and add contextual links pointing to your newest money pages.
* [ ] Find pages ranking in positions **#4 to #15** and boost them with 5–10 exact/partial-match internal links from related cluster posts.
* [ ] Check for broken internal links (404s) and redirect chains (301 hops).
* [ ] Ensure new articles link out to at least 3–5 existing cluster pages before hitting publish.
