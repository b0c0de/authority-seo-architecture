# 03. EEAT & YMYL Checklist for Finance & Affiliate Sites 🛡️

In high-stakes niches like **Finance, Fintech, SaaS, and Affiliate Reviews (YMYL – Your Money or Your Life)**, Google evaluates trust, firsthand experience, and editorial accountability far more strictly than in general niches.

Use this checklist to audit every money page and review before publishing.

---

### 1. Experience & Firsthand Testing Signals (The "E" in EEAT)

* [ ] **Original Visual Proof:** Include original screenshots, custom comparison tables, dashboard walkthroughs, or product testing photos instead of generic stock images.
* [ ] **Testing Methodology Section:** Clearly explain *how* you tested, evaluated, or ranked the financial products or tools (e.g., *"We tested 14 business banking accounts over 60 days based on fees, wire speed, and API integrations"*).
* [ ] **Specific Pros, Cons & Dealbreakers:** Avoid generic marketing fluff. Explicitly state who the product is *not* for.

---

### 2. Expertise & Author Accountability

* [ ] **Verified Author Bio Box:** Every article must have a real, identifiable author with a dedicated author archive page, credentials, and links to LinkedIn/social profiles.
* [ ] **Fact-Checked / Reviewed By Tag:** For finance, tax, or legal-adjacent content, include a secondary reviewer or editorial fact-check timestamp (*"Fact-checked by [Name], CPA / Financial Analyst"*).
* [ ] **Author Schema Markup:** Implement structured `Person` schema (`sameAs`, `jobTitle`, `knowsAbout`) connected to `Article` / `Review` schema.

---

### 3. Authoritativeness & Editorial Transparency

* [ ] **Clear Affiliate Disclosure:** Place a visible, plain-English disclosure above the fold before the first affiliate link.
* [ ] **Editorial Guidelines & Review Process Pages:** Maintain dedicated, linked pages in your footer explaining your editorial standards, monetization model, and correction policy.
* [ ] **Primary Source Citations:** Link out to authoritative primary sources (central banks, SEC filings, official fee schedules, peer-reviewed data) rather than citing secondary blogs.

---

### 4. Trustworthiness & Technical Hygiene

* [ ] **Visible "Last Updated" Date:** Display accurate update timestamps when rates, APYs, pricing, or terms change (never fake freshness dates without real updates).
* [ ] **Proper Link Attributes:** Mark all commercial/affiliate outbound links with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` while keeping editorial citations and internal links clean.
* [ ] **HTTPS, Clear Contact Info & Physical Address:** Ensure your Contact and About pages list real company details, support email, and team leadership.
